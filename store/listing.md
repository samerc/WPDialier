## English (en-US) — default

**Title:** Dialer 8 — Metro phone app

**Short description:**
The flat, fast phone app you remember. Tiles, big type, zero clutter. No ads.

**Full description:**

Dialer 8 brings the beloved flat "Metro" design back to your phone — a full
replacement phone app inspired by the classic Windows Phone 8 look, rebuilt
for modern Android.

**A real phone app**
• Works as your default phone app: dialing, in-call screen, call history
• Big, glanceable typography — no clutter, no ads, ever
• T9 smart dial: type 227 and see everyone named "Bas…" instantly
• Speed dial tiles and a people list with fast alphabet jumping
• Dual SIM aware: pick a SIM per call, per contact, or set a default
• Call blocking and decline-with-text-message with your own quick replies
• Rich contact profiles: numbers, emails, dates, notes — plus one-tap
  actions for your messaging apps (WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, and more)

**Made to feel right**
• Authentic flat design: tiles, pivots, and the press-tilt effect
• Dark and light themes with your choice of accent color
• One-handed mode: lists anchor to the bottom, within thumb reach
• Works with hardware keypads and flip phones (D-pad friendly)
• English, French, and Arabic — with full right-to-left support

**Private by design**
Everything stays on your phone. Dialer 8 collects nothing, uploads nothing,
and shows no ads. No account, no analytics, no tracking — read the privacy
policy. The whole app is under 4 MB.

Dialer 8 is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by
Microsoft.
