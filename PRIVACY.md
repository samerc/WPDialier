# Privacy Policy — Dialer 8

**Effective date:** August 4, 2026
**Developer contact:** fancyshark505@gmail.com

Dialer 8 is a phone (dialer) application styled after Windows Phone 8. It is
designed to work entirely on your device.

## The short version

Dialer 8 does **not** collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any of your
personal data. Everything the app processes — your contacts, call history,
and settings — stays on your phone. We operate no servers and receive no
data from the app.

## Data the app accesses, and why

To function as your phone app, Dialer 8 requests the following Android
permissions. All processing happens locally on your device:

| Data / permission | Why the app needs it |
|---|---|
| Phone / place and manage calls | Making and answering calls; showing the in-call screen |
| Contacts (read/write) | Showing your contacts, editing them, saving new ones to the account you choose (device, Google, Outlook, ...) |
| Call log (read/write) | Showing your call history and adding entries for calls you make |
| Phone state | Detecting your SIM cards so calls use the SIM you prefer |
| Notifications | Showing incoming, ongoing, and missed call notifications |
| Vibration | Haptic feedback on the keypad (optional, can be turned off) |
| Location (optional) | Only used if you tap "use current location" or the map when adding an address to a contact. Never accessed otherwise |
| Internet | Only used to load map tiles from OpenStreetMap if you open the map picker. The app has no other network features |

## What the app does NOT do

- No analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind
- No accounts, sign-ups, or cloud sync operated by us
- No data leaves your device to us — we have no servers
- No sale or sharing of personal information

## Third parties

- **OpenStreetMap** — if you open the optional map picker when editing a
  contact address, map tiles are fetched from OpenStreetMap's servers. Like
  any web request, this exposes your IP address to them. See the
  [OSMF privacy policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).
- **Your contact sync accounts** — when you save a contact to an account
  (e.g. Google or Outlook), that account's own service syncs it under its
  own privacy policy, exactly as it would with any contacts app.

## Data retention and deletion

All app data lives on your device. Deleting a contact, clearing call
history, or uninstalling the app removes the corresponding data. The app
keeps no copy anywhere else.

## Children

Dialer 8 does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone,
including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this document will be updated and
the effective date revised before the change ships.
